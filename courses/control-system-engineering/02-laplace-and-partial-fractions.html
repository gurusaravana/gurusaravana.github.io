Notes

The unilateral Laplace transform turns linear differential equations in time into algebraic equations in the complex frequency $s = \sigma + j\omega$:

$$\mathcal L\{f(t)\} = F(s) = \int_{0^-}^{\infty} f(t)e^{-st}\,dt$$

Partial-fraction expansion

To invert $F(s) = N(s)/D(s)$ with the degree of $N$ less than that of $D$ (otherwise divide first), split it into simple terms.

Case 1: real and distinct roots. $F(s) = \dfrac{K_1}{s+p_1} + \dots + \dfrac{K_n}{s+p_n}$ with $K_i = \lim_{s\to-p_i}(s+p_i)F(s)$.

Case 2: a repeated real root $-p_1$ of multiplicity $r$: include $\dfrac{K_1}{(s+p_1)^r} + \dfrac{K_2}{(s+p_1)^{r-1}} + \dots + \dfrac{K_r}{s+p_1}$, with

$$K_i = \frac{1}{(i-1)!}\lim_{s\to-p_1}\frac{d^{\,i-1}}{ds^{\,i-1}}\left[(s+p_1)^rF(s)\right]$$

Case 3: complex-conjugate roots from $s^2 + as + b$: use $\dfrac{K_2s + K_3}{s^2+as+b}$ and balance coefficients, or complex residues $K_2$, $K_2^*$. The pair inverts to a damped sinusoid $Ce^{-\alpha t}\cos(\beta t - \phi)$.

Use the partial fractions explorer below: switch between the three cases and watch how each term adds up to $f(t)$.

Worked example: transfer function from a differential equation

Find the transfer function of $\dfrac{dc(t)}{dt} + 2c(t) = r(t)$, where $c$ is the output and $r$ the input.

  1. The transfer function is $G(s) = C(s)/R(s)$ with all initial conditions zero.
  2. Transform both sides and use linearity: $\mathcal L\{dc/dt\} + 2C(s) = R(s)$.
  3. Differentiation theorem: $\mathcal L\{dc/dt\} = sC(s) - c(0^-)$.
  4. Zero initial conditions: $sC(s) + 2C(s) = R(s)$.
  5. Factor: $(s+2)C(s) = R(s)$.
  6. So $G(s) = \dfrac{C(s)}{R(s)} = \dfrac{1}{s+2}$, a first-order system with time constant 0.5 s (see Lesson 7).

Exercises

  1. Find $f(t)$ for $F(s) = \dfrac{1}{(s+1)(s+2)}$.
    Show solution
    $K_1 = 1/(−1+2) = 1$, $K_2 = 1/(−2+1) = −1$. $f(t) = e^{-t} − e^{-2t}$. Check it in the explorer with $a = 1$, $b = 2$.
  2. Find $f(t)$ for $F(s) = \dfrac{2}{(s+1)^2(s+2)}$.
    Show solution
    $K_1 = 2/(−1+2) = 2$, $K_2 = \frac{d}{ds}\left[\frac{2}{s+2}\right]_{s=-1} = −2$, $K_3 = 2/(−2+1)^2 = 2$. $f(t) = 2te^{-t} − 2e^{-t} + 2e^{-2t}$.
  3. Find $f(t)$ for $F(s) = \dfrac{3}{s^2 + 2s + 5}$.
    Show solution
    $s^2 + 2s + 5 = (s+1)^2 + 2^2$, so $f(t) = \frac32 e^{-t}\sin 2t$.
  4. Use the final value theorem on $Y(s) = \dfrac{10}{s(s+2)}$.
    Show solution
    $y(\infty) = \lim_{s\to0} s\cdot\frac{10}{s(s+2)} = 5$.

Practice problems from the textbook

From the end-of-chapter Problems in N. S. Nise, Control Systems Engineering, 7th ed. (Hoboken, NJ: John Wiley & Sons, 2015), Chapter 2 (Modeling in the Frequency Domain). “Nise 4.24” means Chapter 4, Problem 24; § is the textbook section the problem practises. Work from the textbook statement and figure. The hints and short answers here are written for this course; full solutions are not reproduced.

  1. Nise 2.1 §2.2 Derive the Laplace transforms of a unit step, a unit ramp, a sine and a cosine from the definition.
    Hint and answer
    Hint. Use $F(s)=\int_0^\infty f(t)e^{-st}dt$. For the ramp, integrate by parts. For sine and cosine, write them with $e^{\pm j\omega t}$.
    Answer. $1/s$, $1/s^2$, $\omega/(s^2+\omega^2)$ and $s/(s^2+\omega^2)$.
  2. Nise 2.2 §2.2 Use the transform tables and theorems to get the transforms of a damped sine, a damped cosine and $t^3$.
    Hint and answer
    Hint. Multiplying by an exponential shifts $s$ (frequency-shift theorem). For $t^n$ use $n!/s^{n+1}$.
    Answer. For $t^3$: $6/s^4$. For the damped sine and cosine, take the answers of Problem 1 and replace $s$ by $s + a$.
  3. Nise 2.4 §2.2 Solve the differential equations of Chapter 1, Problem 20 again, this time with Laplace transforms.
    Hint
    Hint. Transform each equation, solve for $X(s)$, expand in partial fractions and invert term by term. Compare with your classical answer.
  4. Nise 2.5 §2.2 Solve the equations of Chapter 1, Problem 21 with Laplace transforms and the given initial conditions.
    Hint
    Hint. Remember $\mathcal{L}[\dot x]=sX(s)-x(0)$ and $\mathcal{L}[\ddot x]=s^2X(s)-sx(0)-\dot x(0)$.
  5. Nise 2.8 §2.3 Find the transfer function $Y(s)/X(s)$ of a third-order differential equation.
    Hint
    Hint. Transform both sides with zero initial conditions, collect $Y(s)$ on the left and $X(s)$ on the right, and divide.
  6. Nise 2.9 §2.3 Write the differential equation for each of three given transfer functions.
    Hint
    Hint. Cross-multiply, then replace each $s^n$ by the $n$-th time derivative.
  7. Nise 2.12 §2.3 Draw a block diagram for a second-order equation with non-zero initial conditions.
    Hint
    Hint. Transform with the initial conditions. The initial-condition terms appear as extra inputs added to the forcing input.
  8. Nise 2.15 §2.3 Use MATLAB to find the partial-fraction expansion of a rational function.
    Hint
    Hint. Use [r, p, k] = residue(num, den), then check one residue by hand with the cover-up rule.
