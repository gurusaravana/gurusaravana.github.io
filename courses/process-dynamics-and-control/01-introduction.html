By the end of this lesson you can

  • Explain why industrial processes need automatic control
  • Identify controlled, manipulated and disturbance variables
  • Draw a feedback loop as a block diagram and read a simple P&ID

Notes

Every plant, from a water treatment unit to a refinery, must keep variables such as level, temperature, pressure and flow close to target values while raw materials, demand and weather keep changing. Process control is the engineering of making that happen automatically, safely and economically.

The three kinds of variables

VariableMeaningExample: home water heater
Controlled variable (CV)What we want to keep at a target (the set-point)Water temperature
Manipulated variable (MV)What the controller is allowed to adjustHeater power
Disturbance variable (DV)What changes the CV but we do not setCold water flow when a tap is opened

The feedback loop

A sensor measures the CV, the controller compares it with the set-point to get the error $e(t) = y_{sp}(t) - y(t)$, and moves the final control element (usually a valve) to reduce the error. Because the action depends on the measured result, the loop corrects disturbances it was never told about.

Control objectives

  • Safety: keep pressures, temperatures and levels inside safe limits.
  • Product quality: hold compositions and properties on specification.
  • Economics: run close to constraints with the least energy and raw material.
  • Environmental protection: keep emissions within permits.

Reading a P&ID

Piping and instrumentation diagrams use circles (bubbles) with letter codes: the first letter is the measured variable (L level, T temperature, F flow, P pressure) and the following letters the function (T transmitter, C controller, I indicator). So LT is a level transmitter and TIC a temperature indicating controller.

Exercises

  1. For a car's cruise control, name the CV, MV and two disturbances.
    Show solution
    CV: vehicle speed. MV: throttle (fuel) position. Disturbances: road gradient, wind, vehicle load.
  2. What do the tags FT-101 and LIC-205 mean on a P&ID?
    Show solution
    FT-101 is flow transmitter number 101. LIC-205 is level indicating controller number 205.
  3. Why can feedback control correct a disturbance that is never measured?
    Show solution
    Because the controller acts on the measured error in the controlled variable. Any disturbance that moves the CV creates an error, and the controller responds to that error whatever caused it.
