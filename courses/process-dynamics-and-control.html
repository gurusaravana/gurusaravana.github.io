About this course

Who it is for

Third-year students in Electronics and Instrumentation, Chemical and Electrical engineering. You should know basic calculus, differential equations and mass balances.

How to use this site

Read the notes before class, try the simulation, then do the exercises. Reveal a solution only after you have tried it yourself. Bring questions to the tutorial.
