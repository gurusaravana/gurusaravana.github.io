Control System Engineering

How to model, analyse and design feedback control systems: from transfer functions and block diagrams to time response, stability, compensation and state space. Nineteen lessons that follow the course plan, with notes, worked exercises and live simulations.

About this course

Who it is for

Third-year B.Tech Electrical and Electronics Engineering students (Semester V). Prerequisite: Signals and Systems. Bring a scientific calculator to every class; MATLAB or Python skills help.

How to use this site

Read the notes before class, try the simulation, then do the exercises. Reveal a solution only after you have tried it yourself. Bring questions to the tutorial.
